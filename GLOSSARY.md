# Film Tracking

A personal, single-user record of the films its owner has watched: what was watched and when, with no ratings, notes or reviews.

## Language

**Film**:
A movie as identified in the external catalogue the app draws from.
_Avoid_: Movie, title

**Watch**:
One viewing of a Film by the owner on a given date; watching a Film again is a new Watch.
_Avoid_: Log, entry, view, diary entry

**Diary**:
The owner's Watches, listed by date.
_Avoid_: Watchlist, history, log
